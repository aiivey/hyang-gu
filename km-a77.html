KM/A-77

A domestically developed advanced fighter aircraft.

Hyang Gu National Industry tactical fighter platform

Specifications Design overview

Advanced tactical capability.

The KM/A-77 is HGNI's domestically developed fighter aircraft for air defense and tactical operations.

The aircraft represents Hyang Gu's investment in modern fighter capability. It is built around a twin-engine configuration with swept wings for efficient transonic performance, a modern avionics suite, and multiple hardpoints for tactical payload flexibility.

The KM/A-77 complements Hyang Gu's unmanned and directed-defense platforms, providing crewed capability for air-to-air intercept, close air support, and homeland defense missions.

Design priorities.

Efficient aerodynamics

Swept-wing geometry and twin-engine layout optimize transonic cruise and acceleration performance while maintaining reasonable field-length requirements.

Payload flexibility

Multiple hardpoints and a modern avionics bus allow rapid configuration changes for different tactical missions without extensive ground conversion.

Domestic integration

Design modularity and use of standardized subsystems support long-term sustainment and domestic upgrade paths as technology matures.

Operational roles.

The KM/A-77 is designed to support multiple operational profiles within Hyang Gu's air defense and tactical framework.

Air interceptDetection, engagement and neutralization of airborne threats
Close supportTactical air-to-ground operations in support of ground forces
Homeland defenseRapid-response capability for airspace sovereignty and protection

Operational employment depends on training, basing infrastructure and integration with broader air defense architecture.
