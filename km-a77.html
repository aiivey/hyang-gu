A fictional advanced aviation program by Hyang Gu National Industries.

Aircraft overview

The KM/A-77 HEL represents Hyang Gu's ambition to establish an advanced domestic crewed-aircraft industry.

Envisioned as a long-range multirole fighter, the HEL is intended to support air defence, patrol, and other fictional air-force missions. Its program emphasizes maintainability, pilot awareness, and long-term domestic aerospace expertise.

Development is planned as a gradual process, building engineering skills and industrial capacity while using carefully managed international partnerships where appropriate.

Design philosophy

Airframe and maintainability

The HEL concept prioritizes a durable airframe, accessible maintenance systems, and modular components intended to simplify long-term support.

Flight systems

The fictional aircraft is envisioned with modern digital flight controls, integrated cockpit displays, and systems intended to help manage pilot workload.

Avionics and awareness

Its proposed avionics suite brings navigation, communications, sensor information, and aircraft-health monitoring together through a unified cockpit interface.

Domestic industry

A major goal is to develop local engineering expertise, component production, software support, quality assurance, and aircraft maintenance capabilities.

Development roadmap

Phase I — Research: Establish the engineering team, define program requirements, and develop a preliminary design.

Phase II — Demonstration: Assess the concept through staged design reviews and fictional test milestones.

Phase III — Industrial readiness: Establish testing, quality-control, technician training, and maintenance infrastructure.

Phase IV — Production planning: Review reliability, cost, and manufacturing readiness before considering any production decision.

Program priorities

The KM/A-77 is intended to strengthen Hyang Gu's aerospace industry beyond a single aircraft project.

Build a sustainable domestic aviation capability through staged research, workforce development, and industrial investment.
