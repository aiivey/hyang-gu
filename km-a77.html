KM/A-77

A domestically developed advanced fighter aircraft.

Hyang Gu National Industry tactical fighter platform

Specifications Design overview

Advanced tactical capability.

The KM/A-77 is HGNI's domestically developed fighter aircraft for air defense and tactical operations.

The aircraft represents Hyang Gu's investment in modern fighter capability. It is built around a twin-engine configuration with delta-canard aerodynamics for supersonic performance, integrated stealth shaping, and a modern avionics suite. Multiple hardpoints and internal weapons bays provide tactical payload flexibility.

The KM/A-77 complements Hyang Gu's unmanned and air-defense platforms, providing crewed capability for air-to-air intercept, close air support, and homeland defense missions.

Design priorities.

Supersonic performance

Delta-canard configuration with twin engines optimizes transonic and supersonic cruise while maintaining reasonable acceleration and turn performance across the combat envelope.

Stealth integration

Blended fuselage design, internal weapons carriage, and angular surfaces reduce radar signature while maintaining structural efficiency and payload capacity.

Domestic integration

Modular avionics and standardized subsystems support long-term sustainment and domestic upgrade paths as technology matures and doctrine evolves.

Operational roles.

The KM/A-77 is designed to support multiple operational profiles within Hyang Gu's air defense and tactical framework.

Air interceptDetection, engagement and neutralization of airborne threats
Close supportTactical air-to-ground operations in support of ground forces
Homeland defenseRapid-response capability for airspace sovereignty and protection

Operational employment depends on training, basing infrastructure and integration with broader air defense architecture.
